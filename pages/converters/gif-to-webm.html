Length

Stickers longer than 3 s are cut at 3 s. Ping-pong plays the selection forward and back, so it doubles the length — keep the selection at 1.5 s or less. A GIF from a video can be up to 60 s long, but GIFs grow fast: keep them short, narrow and at a low frame rate.

Crop

Drag a box on the preview to crop. Its longer side becomes the sticker's 512 px (Telegram wants one side exactly 512 px), so a smaller box zooms in. Boxes under 512 px get enlarged and look softer.

Telegram shows every sticker in the same square, so a wide sticker looks small and a square one big. A smaller size adds transparent space, which shrinks the sticker and lets you move it towards a side. Square 512 pads it to a full square; Wide 512 pads only left and right, so the sticker gets smaller without taking up more height in the chat.

Size

The server lowers the quality step by step until the file fits the limit. Long or busy clips may not fit; trim them shorter.
