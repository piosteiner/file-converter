Cut

Exact or copy

Exact re-encodes the clip, so it starts and ends where you set it, at near the original quality. Copy keeps the recording's data untouched and is instant, but a clip can only start on a keyframe: it may begin a few seconds early. With a keyframe interval of 1 s in OBS (Settings → Output), copy cuts start within a second of your start.

Smaller files

Lowering the resolution or frame rate keeps the picture sharp at a lower bit rate. The size shown is an estimate from the bit rate; busy scenes need more, still ones less.
